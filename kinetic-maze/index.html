Kinetic Maze


Last updated January 06, 2026.

Put your logic skills to the test in Kinetic Maze, a clever puzzle game where you must guide the right pieces into the target hole by sliding them across the board. Sounds simple? Each level adds new twists, tricky layouts, and obstacles that will challenge your strategy and precision.

Whether you're a casual gamer or a puzzle pro, Kinetic Maze offers satisfying gameplay that’s easy to pick up but hard to master.

🧠 Features:

- Intuitive sliding mechanics with smooth controls

- 41 handcrafted puzzles with increasing difficulty

- Minimalist design for a clean and focused experience

- Play offline anytime, anywhere

- Perfect for short sessions or extended play

- Great for all ages — train your brain daily!
